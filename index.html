A little about me

My name is Alex Naz, a fourth-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) student at STI College Legazpi, originally from Guinobatan, Albay, Philippines. I have a strong interest in technology, creativity, and digital design, and I enjoy exploring new ideas, learning different tools, and continuously improving my technical and creative abilities. Throughout my academic journey, I have developed an appreciation for how technology and design can work together to create practical, visually appealing, and user-friendly solutions. I aspire to become a professional UI/UX Designer, where I can combine my creativity and technical knowledge to design meaningful digital experiences. I aim to further develop my skills in user interface design, user experience principles, wireframing, and prototyping while keeping up with modern design trends and technologies. Through hands-on projects and real-world experiences, I hope to build a strong portfolio, gain valuable industry experience, and create websites and applications that prioritize usability, accessibility, and user satisfaction.
